Our Mission

ANCHORED
IN CHRIST

Rock Solid Man exists to call men back to the foundation — Jesus Christ. We gather monthly for worship, a message, and brotherhood, committed to building men who are solid in their faith, their families, and their communities.

We help men build unshakable strength — physically, mentally, and spiritually. Through proven strategies, expert guidance, and a community of iron-sharpening-iron brotherhood, we empower men to stand firm.

Whether it's battling addiction, growing in faith, or stepping into leadership, we believe every man has the potential to be Rock Solid — anchored in Christ and unmovable.

What We Believe

OUR
FOUNDATION

The Word & The Trinity

  • THE BIBLE is the divinely inspired, inerrant, infallible, sufficient Word of God.
  • ONE GOD eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • JESUS CHRIST is fully God and fully man. We believe in His virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, vicarious and atoning death through His shed blood, bodily resurrection, ascension to the right hand of the Father, and ultimate personal return in power and glory.
2 Timothy 3:16-17 | Matthew 28:19 | 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

Salvation & The Holy Spirit

  • SALVATION and regeneration by the Holy Spirit is essential for all people. It comes through repentance of sin and belief in Jesus Christ's death on our behalf. This is the only way to a personal relationship with a Holy God.
  • THE HOLY SPIRIT indwells every believer upon profession of faith, enabling the Christian to live a godly life.
  • MARRIAGE is the uniting of one man and woman in covenant commitment for a lifetime.
  • LEADERSHIP — a father must manage his household well. (1 Timothy 3:4a)
John 14:6 | Romans 10:9 | Genesis 2:24
