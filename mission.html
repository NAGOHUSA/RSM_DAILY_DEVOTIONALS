Built on the Rock · Matthew 7:24

OUR
MISSION

"Everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock." — Matthew 7:24

01

OUR VISION

To cultivate a multi-generational, multi-denominational, and multi-cultural community of men seeking to build their lives on the Rock of Jesus.

02

OUR MISSION

To model our vision through the building of strong relationships where men sharpen each other as iron sharpens iron. This is accomplished by walking in bold leadership, rooted in sacrificial love.

03

OUR PURPOSE

Rock Solid Man Inc. exists to disciple and build men up to be followers of Christ.

  • Preach God's Word
  • Spread the knowledge of the Gospel
  • Teach and encourage men to be sacrificial leaders
  • Grow in relational community with men who seek to follow Christ
  • Grow the community of Christian men by removing barriers to entry such as denomination, culture, and age
  • Teach men how to build their lives on the Solid Rock of Jesus
The Foundation
"As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another."
— Proverbs 27:17

A brotherhood of men anchored in Christ, rooted in community, and committed to building on the Rock.

04

STRATEGIC PILLARS

Four pillars that shape everything we do as a brotherhood.

Pillar 01

DISCIPLESHIP

Building men up to follow Jesus, know His Word, and live it out.

Pillar 02

WORSHIP

Honoring God together through prayer, praise, and lives surrendered to Christ.

Pillar 03

FELLOWSHIP

Forming strong relationships where men sharpen, support, and encourage one another.

Pillar 04

SERVICE

Putting faith into action by serving our families, churches, and communities with sacrificial love.

05

OUR CULTURE

We are a brotherhood of honest, accountable, encouraging men who reject passivity, pursue holiness, carry one another's burdens, and challenge each other to grow.
